Privacy Policy

How functions.io collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last Updated: November 2024

1. Introduction

functions.io ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at functions.io and any associated subdomains or pages.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using our website, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect includes:

  • Personal Data: Personally identifiable information, such as your first name, last name, and email address, that you voluntarily provide to us when contacting us through our website contact forms or via direct email.
  • Derivative Data & Analytics: Information our servers or analytics providers automatically collect when you access the site, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, access times, and the pages you viewed directly before and after accessing the site.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries, contact form submissions, and support requests.
  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website and consulting services.
  • Analyze usage trends and improve website performance and user experience.
  • Maintain the safety, security, and integrity of our systems and services.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our website and hold certain information.

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics (gtag.js) to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics collects information anonymously to report website trends without identifying individual visitors.
  • Cookie Consent: We use CookieYes to manage user cookie consent preferences in compliance with applicable data privacy laws. You can customize or withdraw your consent at any time through our cookie consent banner.

5. Third-Party Processing

When you submit a contact form on our website, your submission is processed via Google Forms infrastructure. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. We employ reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by functions.io. If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@functions.io.
